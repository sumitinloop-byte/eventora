Frequently asked questions

Is registration free?

Registering is free. Some events have a ticket price shown on the event card; free events are marked “Free”.

Where is my registration saved?

Registrations are saved in your browser's Local Storage and listed on the “My Tickets” page.

Can I cancel a registration?

Yes. Open “My Tickets” and press Cancel on any booking.

Can I add an event to my calendar?

Yes. Use the “Add to calendar” button on any event page to download an .ics file.
